# 🎯 Vision  
SIRIUS Local AI evolves into a **System‑Intelligent Autonomous Workstation** capable of operating Windows 11 safely, deterministically, explainably, identity‑aware, autonomy‑aware, and fully offline.

The roadmap is divided into **three eras**:

1. **Runtime Era (5.x)** — deterministic foundation  
2. **Autonomy Era (6.x)** — supervised autonomous operation  
3. **System Intelligence Era (7.x)** — predictive, self‑optimizing workstation
